Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information SmartChat AI ("we", "the app") collects when you use smartchat-ai-ten.vercel.app, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you sign up with email, we store your name, email address, and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Chats. The messages you send, the AI responses, chat titles, and when chats were created, updated, or pinned.
  • Technical data. Session data needed to keep you signed in, and standard request information such as IP address, which is used for security and rate limiting.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, sign you in, and verify your email address.
  • To store your chat history so you can return to it later.
  • To generate AI responses and chat titles.
  • To prevent abuse, for example by limiting how many requests can be sent in a short time.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, we only request basic profile information (name, email address, and profile picture). This information is used solely to create and identify your SmartChat AI account. We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or any other Google data, and we do not share Google user data with third parties except the service providers listed below that are needed to run the app.

Service providers

We rely on the following providers to operate the app:

  • Neon provides authentication (Neon Auth) and the PostgreSQL database where your account and chats are stored.
  • Google (Gemini API) processes the messages you send, along with recent messages from the same chat, to generate responses and chat titles. Depending on the service tier used, Google may use this content to improve its products, as described in the Gemini API terms. Please don't include sensitive personal information in your messages.
  • Vercel hosts the website.

Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and similar storage only to keep you signed in and to remember which chat you had open. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Data retention and deletion

  • Your chats are kept until you delete them. Deleting a chat permanently removes its messages.
  • Email sign-ups that are never verified are automatically deleted after 24 hours, as long as they have no chats or other activity.
  • To delete your account and all associated data, email us at the address below and we will process the request.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections, passwords are stored hashed, and each user can only access their own chats. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Children

SmartChat AI is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
